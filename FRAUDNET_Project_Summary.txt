FRAUDNET - Fraud Detection System
=================================
Date: 2026-08-18
Database: fraudnet_db (MySQL 8.0)

TABLES CREATED:
1. customer - Customer information
2. account - Bank accounts
3. financial_transaction - All transactions
4. location - Geographic locations
5. device - Device/IP tracking
6. suspicious_transaction - Fraud alerts
7. risk_score - Account risk levels
8. fraud_case - Investigation cases
9. account_relationship - Account connections

FRAUD DETECTION RULES:
1. RAPID_TRANSACTION - Multiple transactions within 10 minutes
2. LARGE_TRANSACTION - Amount > 5x account average
3. SHARED_DEVICE - Device used by multiple customers
4. CIRCULAR_TRANSFER - Money returns to origin account

REPORTS GENERATED:
1. suspicious_transactions.csv - All fraud alerts
2. risk_scores.csv - Account risk levels
3. daily_summary.csv - Daily transaction summary
4. high_risk_accounts.csv - Accounts needing attention
5. circular_transfers.csv - Circular money movement

KEY FINDINGS:
- Total transactions: [run: SELECT COUNT(*) FROM financial_transaction;]
- Suspicious transactions: [run: SELECT COUNT(*) FROM suspicious_transaction;]
- High-risk accounts: [run: SELECT COUNT(*) FROM risk_score WHERE risk_level IN ('HIGH','VERY HIGH','CRITICAL');]
